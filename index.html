What's this about?

CorpsTools is a collection of various websites, applications and tools made by the Corps of Cadets.

What? The goal of this website is to create an easily accessible hub of tools to assist Cadets in their daily lives. This could be academic tools, leadership tools, physical fitness tools, information hubs, creative applications, media platforms, etc...

How? The Corps of Cadets contains a large pool of talented people that are capable of implementing their own ideas. We want to:

  • Create a platform to promote these projects to the entire Corps.
  • Create a platform for discussion between Cadet Clients and Cadet Developers to efficiently find and fix bugs and issues.
  • Create a system for implementing and integrating these projects at the Corps-level.

How can I help? If you have a project that you would like to submit to us or you want to help us implement projects, contact CDT Korbin Deary at korbin.deary@westpoint.edu.

What if I just have a good idea? If you have a great idea but don't have the resources to build it, feel free to contact our team.
