Mission

To empower the Corps of Cadets

Through innovative, cadet-driven technological solutions that enhance daily operations, foster leadership, and streamline cadet life.

Vision

Centralized Distribution Hub

We bridge the gap between Cadets and Cadet Developers by identifying operational challenges, developing robust software solutions, and seamlessly integrating them at the Corps-level.

Two Pillars of Excellence

The Steering Committee

The outward-facing branch responsible for product management, user research, and client relations. They engage with the Corps to identify pain points and gather feedback.

  • Steering Commander: Overall organization lead and advocate.
  • Cadet Liaisons: Identifying daily challenges and feedback.
  • Design Volunteers: Visualizing ideas through wireframes and testing.

The Technical Team

The inward-facing branch responsible for engineering, maintenance, and deployment. They turn requirements into functional, secure software.

  • Executive Officer (XO): Technical lead and operations manager.
  • Project Maintainers: Adopting and guiding specific app codebases.
  • Volunteer Developers: Building and deploying code on an asynchronous basis.

How We Build

1. Ideation & Discovery

Identifying problems or receiving pitches from Cadet Clients.

2. Scoping

Reviewing ideas for feasibility, impact, and resources by the leadership team.

3. Development

Iterative building by our Technical Team of volunteer software engineers.

4. Testing & Feedback

Beta-testing and gathering feedback from the Steering Committee and the Corps.

5. Deployment & Promotion

Deploying the tool and promoting it to ensure it reaches those who need it most.
